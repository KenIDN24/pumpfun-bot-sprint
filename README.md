Dip Sniper paper-trading bot for pump.fun bonding curves (agent-fun/replay/v2). It buys the dip after a proven 2x pump — a coin that ran >= 2x from its first price and retraced to <= 60% of its peak, with fresh buy flow in its last 10 trades — and exits on a -12% stop-loss, a trailing stop armed at +50%, or a 1,800-slot time stop. Dev grade (grade-local, process driver): -0.01397 SOL over 88 round trips on 88 distinct mints, max drawdown 2.11% — essentially breakeven, a lottery ticket for the unseen 6h final window.

## Run it

```sh
node main.ts
```
